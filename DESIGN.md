# Glad Style Fashion design direction

This file is the shared design brief for the storefront. Edit it whenever the brand direction changes. Concrete instructions here should describe the desired experience, while screenshots and reference links should be treated as inspiration unless a section explicitly says to reproduce it.

## Brand

- Name: Glad Style Fashion
- Market: Lagos, United Kingdom, and United States
- Positioning: polished, feminine ready-to-wear clothing that feels special but remains easy to buy and wear
- Primary accent: the deep pink used in the dotted `G` logo
- Core neutrals: white and black
- Logo: pink dotted `G`, black `glad style` wordmark, and black script `fashion` on white

## Visual direction

- Clean fashion-editorial presentation with generous space and strong garment photography
- Feminine and confident, without decorative clutter
- Product imagery should use the same pale blush architectural studio background whenever possible
- Keep garment colors accurate and allow the clothing to carry most of the visual interest
- Prefer sharp, restrained layouts and subtle interaction feedback

## Typography

- Use the logo artwork for the brand wordmark
- Keep navigation and utility text clean, compact, and readable
- Product titles should feel editorial while product details remain practical
- Avoid overly ornate display typography outside the logo

## Storefront priorities

1. Make the current ready-made collection immediately visible.
2. Show price, available sizes, fabric, fit, and delivery information clearly.
3. Make shopping and checkout easy on mobile.
4. Establish trust for Lagos, UK, and US customers.
5. Use one clear primary action per section.

## Current product photography system

- Primary background: pale blush plaster with a soft architectural arch
- Lighting: soft daylight from the left
- Framing: full-body portrait with the entire hem visible
- Model direction: adult Black women with natural styling and realistic skin texture
- Product fidelity: preserve garment color, print, neckline, sleeves, seams, tiers, length, and fabric behavior
- Product pages should never present an AI image as proof of exact measurements or fit

## Pages

### Home

- Strong collection image and direct shop action
- Product grid focused on available ready-made pieces
- Short, true brand story
- Delivery and shopping reassurance

### Product

- Large garment image
- Product name and price
- Size selection and useful fit guidance
- Fabric, care, stock, dispatch, delivery, and exchange information

### Checkout

- Minimal distractions
- Clear delivery destination and cost
- Verified payment status and an accurate confirmation message

## Avoid

- Generic stock fashion photography
- Unverified claims, reviews, or delivery promises
- Multiple unrelated accent colors
- Heavy gradients, glass effects, or decorative animations
- Changing product construction in generated images
